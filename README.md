📊 Project Overview

An interactive Customer Churn & Retention Analytics project built using Python and Power BI. The project analyzes customer behavior, churn patterns, tenure, subscription types, customer value, revenue, and payment methods to identify factors affecting customer retention.

🎯 Business Objectives
Analyze overall customer churn
Identify factors contributing to customer churn
Analyze churn by contract and subscription type
Understand churn across customer characteristics
Analyze churn against tenure and monthly charges
Calculate Customer Lifetime Value
Analyze revenue by state
Identify customer segments requiring retention strategies

🔍 Key Business Insights

The analysis was used to identify:

Overall customer churn rate and churned customer count
Contract types with higher churn
Subscription types with higher customer value
Payment methods associated with higher churn
State-wise revenue performance
Relationship between customer tenure and churn
High-value customer segments
💡 Business Recommendations
Focus retention strategies on high-churn customer segments.
Investigate the reasons behind churn in specific contract types.
Prioritize retention of high-value customers.
Monitor payment methods and customer service factors linked with churn.
Use customer lifetime value to identify valuable customers for targeted retention campaigns.
